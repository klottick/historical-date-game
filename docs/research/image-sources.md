### NASA AI-attribution boundary — saved official policy

The saved `raw/terms-nasa.html:1671` says NASA cannot verify information incorporated into a large language model and that “attribution of the information directly to NASA is not permitted.” Its **factual source disclosure** rule at line 1675 says: “As a statement of fact, you can acknowledge your AI tool includes NASA source material, but do not imply any review or permission was granted by NASA for its specific use.” At line 1683: “All outputs from the AI model should be attributed to the AI product itself, not NASA.” Also: "References like “according to NASA” or similar are prohibited in AI products."

The same line 1671 states: “Similarly, NASA does not permit our Insignia (i.e., NASA Meatball, Worm logotype, or Seal) to appear with AI generated imagery or in the training of AI tools.” NASA strongly encourages marking AI-generated products as such; its accuracy disclaimer says NASA is not responsible for information generated by an AI model (`:1679`), and using NASA content to train models does not imply endorsement (`:1687`).

The historical distinction between **human editorial sourcing** (checking an original source and documenting it), factual disclosure of a tool's source material, and **AI-output attribution** remains important. These research citations identify consulted saved pages, not NASA verification or permission. A hypothetical human-written route might be treated differently, but neither human review nor a Commons license is assumed to remove the restrictions. Future direct NASA adoption would require a separate scope/rights decision, potentially **owner/legal review**, not a gate for the selected beta path. NASA remains unselected; unresolved NASA-derived rights/credit ambiguity uses fallback or quarantine, not a human clearance queue. This is not legal clearance.

## Date leakage, sensitivity and adaptation

Fifteen rows carry `date_leak_risk`, including maps, plaques, title pages and founding documents; two flag years in filenames. Machine-check original/derivative bytes, names, captions, credits, alt text and optional metadata. Conservatively exclude text-heavy maps/documents/title pages/plaques and uncertain date-bearing imagery unless automated analysis establishes answer-safe presentation; uncertain cases use original SVG fallbacks, not manual inspection. Pre-answer delivery must not expose the unanswered year. **Do not suppress mandatory attribution to hide the answer**: if compliant credits/crops cannot avoid leakage, use the fallback. Alt text remains useful and truthful without the year. Licensing is not a reason to leak the answer or remove required credits.

Battle art may contain gore, weapons, reenactments or colonial propaganda; portraits of death/assassination subjects are generally less graphic than death scenes. Machine-check sensitive-event, violence, nudity, racist-imagery, colonial/Indigenous-context and identity/publicity metadata; absent or ambiguous safety/relevance evidence uses fallback rather than an inferred pass. Modern art/film/book covers need underlying-work rights, not merely a photographer license. Cropping cannot cure unclear ownership, an incorrect edition or a weak match. Unknown territorial/work/reproduction basis uses fallback; uploader labels and automated tags are not legal opinions or guaranteed detection of unsafe content.

For CC BY: retain author/source, license link/version and modification notice in accessible credits. For CC BY-SA: additionally license relevant adaptations compatibly and retain downstream conditions; do not assume share-alike automatically licenses the whole application, or that it has no effect. CC0/PD credits remain desirable provenance. Machine validation must enforce the selected per-file rights/credits/adaptation policy and use fallback when obligations cannot be honored or their scope is unknown. Trademarks, personality/privacy and culturally restricted material are not cleared merely by open copyright. No human/legal sign-off is a beta publication gate; machine checks are not legal certification.

## Selected caching and immutable media process

In C4, download eligible originals once within limits and machine-validate MIME/size/decoded pixel limits, every redirect/target, Commons repository origin and file rights; failed downloads or checks use fallbacks. Retain original revision URL, author/credit, exact license/basis, metadata snapshot, upstream SHA-1, local SHA-256 and true retrieval time. Generate size-limited derivatives only where rights permit; strip optional EXIF/answer-bearing names, never mandatory credit. Record crops/changes and derivative digests; machine-check answer safety, alt text and credits. Use opaque local asset identifiers; keep raw metadata private. AFTER freezing the complete MediaCatalog batch, revalidate content plus media and emit final `content_media` ValidationReceipts under `automated-v1`, bound to candidate/source digests and the canonical full catalog digest (all rights/creator/credit/alt/fit/source revision/filename/asset SHA fields, including fallbacks). C3's passed `content` receipt with no media digest is never publication-ready. Catalog/rights changes require refreezing and recomputing receipts for all events, not reusing C3 pass flags. C5 independently validates the current catalog digest and hashes actual image/fallback bytes against every referenced current record SHA at publication and release verification; a receipt digest alone is not asset integrity. Metadata or bytes changing invalidates final receipt/verification even when event/media/fallback references stay unchanged. Changing `review_status` to `approved` alone is not acceptance. Preserve immutable manifests and old run assets; no live hotlinks or silent replacement. No human review queue, commissioning or paid curation is introduced.

The current research allowlist contains no Met/NASA/Smithsonian host. Expanding it requires a reviewed proposal and approval, not an importer workaround. Own packaged assets need no external media hostname.

## Retrieval and automated-processing effort

Actual cached raw research responses totaled ~22.3 MB (facts, terms and thumbnails); the thumbnail subset was ~6.06 MB. All reads used public access, no payments or provisioning. The contact-sheet browser MCP could not launch a sandboxed Chromium in this container; installed local Chromium rendered cached data-URI sheets with `--no-sandbox`, with no application changes or logged-in browsing. Render evidence/commands are indexed in the C1 report.

No measured automated acceptance, throughput or final fallback demand exists yet. All events need meaningful fallback mappings; conservative metadata/rights/safety checks may produce substantially more fallbacks than the historical 19% replacement screen, particularly for battles, literature and politics. Report actual machine picture/fallback yield and reason codes instead of promising recognition, legal suitability or full photographic coverage. Engineering, deterministic SVG generation, checks, caching/storage and maintenance are real effort, not a zero-effort promise. **Superseded optional manual-process estimates only:** former 130–265 media/study hours and art commissioning/reviewer labor are not selected beta work or required gates.

For rough sizing only, 0.5–2 MB per original plus 0.1–0.3 MB per derivative would imply ~0.66–2.53 GB for 1,100 full-image pairs before backups; actual size depends on accepted assets versus SVG fallbacks and must be measured. Delivery cost depends on play volume/hosting; no hosting, paid source access, art commissioning or legal service is provisioned. Separate hosting/budget/deployment authorization remains unchanged, not a per-record content gate.
